SCENARIO BASED 
1. Imagine you're the new kid at school and you don't know anyone. How would you feel? What would you want other kids to do to make you feel welcome?"  

- How would you feel?  I'd probably feel nervous, a little scared, and maybe even lonely.  It would be hard not knowing anyone and being in a new environment.
- What would you want other kids to do? I'd want someone to smile at me, maybe say hello, or even just invite me to sit with them at lunch.  It would make me feel less alone and more welcome.
 
2.In the story, why did the character react that way to the loss of their pet?  How would you feel if you experienced a similar loss?"  

- Why did the character react that way?  Losing a pet is like losing a family member.  They probably felt a deep sense of sadness, grief, and maybe even anger.  Pets provide companionship, love, and loyalty, so their loss can be very painful.
- How would you feel?  I'd probably feel heartbroken.  I'd miss my pet's presence, their playful nature, and the comfort they brought me.  It would be a big loss.

3. After volunteering at the homeless shelter, what did you learn about the challenges faced by people experiencing homelessness? How did this experience change your perspective, and what actions could you take to support them in the future?" 

- What did you learn?  I learned that homelessness is a complex issue with many contributing factors, like poverty, mental health challenges, and lack of affordable housing.  It's not just about being lazy or making bad choices.  People experiencing homelessness are often resilient and resourceful, but they face significant obstacles.
- How did it change your perspective?  It made me realize that I take many things for granted, like having a roof over my head and food on the table.  It also made me want to be more compassionate and understanding towards those less fortunate.
- What actions could you take?  I could volunteer more, donate to organizations that support the homeless, or advocate for policies that address the root causes of homelessness.

4. The documentary showed the impact of climate change on indigenous communities. How does understanding their experiences inform your understanding of climate change as a global issue? What are the ethical implications of inaction in the face of such suffering?" 

- How does understanding their experiences inform your understanding?  Indigenous communities are often on the front lines of climate change, experiencing its impacts more acutely than others.  Their stories highlight the urgency of the issue and the devastating consequences of inaction.  It's a reminder that climate change is not just about rising temperatures, but also about human suffering and displacement.
- What are the ethical implications of inaction?  Ignoring the suffering of indigenous communities due to climate change is ethically wrong.  We have a responsibility to act, not just for our own sake, but for the sake of those most vulnerable to its effects.
 

5.During the conflict simulation, you were assigned to mediate a disagreement between two employees.  How did you approach the situation, considering the emotional needs of both individuals? What strategies did you use to build understanding and find a solution?"

- How did you approach the situation?  I tried to listen to both employees' perspectives without judgment, acknowledging their feelings and concerns.  I focused on finding common ground and understanding the root cause of the conflict.
- What strategies did you use?  I used active listening, paraphrasing to ensure understanding, and asking clarifying questions.  I encouraged them to express their feelings and work together to find a solution that addressed both of their needs.
